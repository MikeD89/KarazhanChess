They were rendered from the original SVGs to 128×128 DXT5-compressed BLP textures for this addon. ## Board themes

Default, Horde and Alliance were made for this addon (flat-coloured squares).

Brown, Blue, Green, Purple and Khaki are flat two-colour boards whose colours match Lichess boards (Khaki is Lichess's `ic`; the others share their names). Only the colour values were reused.

## Other textures

The legal-move dot and legal-capture corners were generated for this addon to match the shapes and colours of Lichess's move indicators (chessground CSS; only the geometry and colour values were reused). They are white and tinted in game. The check glow (`check.blp`) is generated by `Tools/textures/check.js` from the gradient values of Lichess's check highlight. The minimap icon predates this file.

## Chess engine

The computer opponent (`KarazhanChess/Engine.lua`) evaluates positions with the PeSTO piece values and piece-square tables by Ronald Friederich (author of the Rofchade engine), as published on the [Chess Programming Wiki](https://www.chessprogramming.org/PeSTO%27s_Evaluation_Function). Only the numbers were reused; the search and the rest of the engine were written for this addon.

## Puzzle data

The puzzles in the `KarazhanChess_Puzzles` addon come from the [Lichess puzzle database](https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles) (`lichess_db_puzzle.csv.zst`, October 2026 export), released into the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). 500,000 puzzles were selected by popularity, number of plays and rating deviation, then re-encoded into a compact format by `Tools/puzzles/build.js`. Puzzle IDs, positions, solutions, ratings and themes are as published by Lichess. Thank you to Lichess and the players whose games and attempts made these puzzles.
